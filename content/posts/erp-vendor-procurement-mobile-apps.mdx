An **ERP mobile app** that copies every desktop screen will not get used. The slice that gets used is **vendor procurement**: suppliers, purchase requests, approvals, and status.

## What belongs on the phone

Raise a request. See if the vendor is approved. Approve a PO. Check delivery. That is the job. Master data, financial close, and 40-column grids stay on the web.

## Why React Native

Procurement happens on site visits and shop floors. React Native can share a TypeScript model with the web ERP while still feeling like a phone app. Weak networks and offline queues matter more than animations.
